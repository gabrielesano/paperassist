## Design Context

### Users
Researchers reading and annotating scientific papers with an existing PDF collection.
They need dependable local storage, repeated-reference reuse, and quick comparison.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, restrained. A practical scholarly desk with clear visual hierarchy.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, warm-neutral workspace, muted teal accents, crisp dividers, compact readable
controls and spacious PDFs. Avoid decorative dashboards, cards, and visual clutter.
Use local assets and an accessible type hierarchy. Desktop reading is the primary context.
These reversible visual choices follow the user's request to decide independently.

### Design Principles
- The document has the most space and attention.
- Every tool clearly acts on the active document.
- Split consultation preserves the primary reading position.
- Uncertain metadata and partial analysis remain visible and correctable.
- Prefer simple, fast interactions and meaningful keyboard focus.
